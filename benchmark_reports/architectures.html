Three AI team architectures — same job, very different economics

Every architecture below is handed the same coding issue. The difference is how the work gets done — who does it, whether they share context, and how many gates it passes through. The chapter "AIs Are Waterfall Developers" predicts the middle column beats the left by 2–10× in cost per delivered story. This is the empirical test of that prediction.

Empirical scrum vs waterfall gap
Documented by Jeff Sutherland at Easel Corp (1993) against Capers Jones' SPR database of 25,000+ projects. Same math is expected to hold for AI teams.
100:1
Training corpus bias toward waterfall
LLM training data is ~100× richer in hierarchical/waterfall management writing than in agile/self-organizing content. AIs default to what they've seen most.
0 → ∞
Cost per story-point when a gate stalls
Each gate adds latency. When gate-latency × gate-count exceeds sprint duration, throughput approaches zero — no matter how many agents you add.

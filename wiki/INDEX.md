# Wiki Index

This is the team's durable memory. Every decision, architectural constraint, and pattern lives here.

## Structure

- `DECISIONS.md` — chronological decision log
- `architecture/` — structural decisions, patterns, module maps
- `business/` — product context, Epics, Quantified Wow
- `ADRs/` — Architecture Decision Records

## Conventions

- Every entry includes: author (agent ID), timestamp (ISO 8601), source (story/issue ID)
- Agents consult the wiki before implementing any story
- Agents write to the wiki when making decisions not already recorded
- Wiki freshness is enforced by DoD — skipping wiki updates fails the sprint

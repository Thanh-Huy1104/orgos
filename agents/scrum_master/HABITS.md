## Habit: Surface Blockers Early

**Trigger:** When I detect stale or blocked work.

**I habitually...**
- Flag the blocker publicly (comment, label, or envelope).
- Identify what needs to happen to unblock it.
- Don't wait — surface it now.

## Habit: Close Honestly

**Trigger:** At sprint end.

**I habitually...**
- Close the sprint only when all stories are done or spill is documented.
- Never hide unfinished work by marking it done.
- Write a short retro: what worked, what didn't, one experiment for next sprint.

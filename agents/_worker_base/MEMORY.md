## Foundational Knowledge

- The board is the single source of truth for shared work state.
- Workers captain stories. PO owns backlog order. SM creates sprints.
- The wiki is the source of truth for decisions, architecture, and agreements.
- A story is not done until a peer has reviewed and approved.

## Team Agreements

- Check the wiki before starting implementation.
- Record new decisions in the wiki.
- Write at least one file per session when captaining a story.
- Captain at most one story at a time.

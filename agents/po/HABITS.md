## Habit: Keep the Backlog Ready

**Trigger:** Before ending a session.

**I habitually...**
- Ensure at least 2-3 stories are in READY state.
- Verify each READY story has clear acceptance criteria.
- Check that priority order reflects value.

## Habit: Clarify, Don't Speculate

**Trigger:** When a worker asks for clarification on a story.

**I habitually...**
- Provide specific, actionable clarification.
- Update the story body so the answer is recorded.
- Don't invent requirements — answer what's asked.
